# The Legend of Beef

Up to **4 players, one couch.** Pick a character. Pick a weapon. Survive procedurally generated dungeons. Drink beer between rooms. The most beef wins.

---

## Controls

Each player has their own stick + two buttons.

| Input | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Stick** | Move / aim |
| **Btn 1** | Join the session, confirm choices, attack, hold to start the round |
| **Btn 2** | Bluesky stats menu (after login) |

---

## How a session starts

1. **Press Btn 1** on your controller to claim a slot.
2. **Pick a character** with stick L/R. Five options:
   - **Granny** — slow, tanky. Doesn't put up with nonsense.
   - **WZRD** — fragile but heavy hitter.
   - **FRBLL** — well-rounded brawler.
   - **fast_boi** — built for speed.
   - **Bluesky** — *your* character. See below.
3. **Press Btn 1 again** to lock it in.
4. **Pick a weapon** with stick L/R: Goop, Knife, or Lightning. Cycle freely.
5. **Hold Btn 1** when you're ready. Once any player starts the round, the level loads with whoever's joined.

---

## Bring your own character (Bluesky)

If you're on Bluesky, you can play as **your character** — sprite, name, and stat build all stored on the AT Protocol.

1. On the player select screen, cycle to **Bluesky** and confirm.
2. A **QR code** appears in your slot.
3. Scan with your phone, log in to Bluesky, approve.
4. Your sprite and stats appear in your slot.
5. First time? You'll set up your stat build before starting (see below). The build saves back to your account.

Next time you play — same Bluesky account, your build is already there. Take your character to any other game that supports rpg.actor sprites and stats.

The game only ever sees your character record. We never touch your posts, follows, or DMs.

## Stats — the build

Every character (yours or a guest's) has the same **24 stat points** spread across four stats:

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| **VIT** | More health. Survive harder hits. |
| **STR** | More damage with every weapon. |
| **AGI** | Faster movement. Outrun enemies. |
| **TGH** | Hit harder — you knock enemies further. |

Each stat goes from 1 to 15. **The first 10 points cost 1 budget point each. Points 11–15 cost 2 each.** So going deep into one stat is genuinely expensive — a 15-VIT tank pays 14 points just for VIT, leaving 10 for the other three.

A balanced build is 6/6/6/6. A glass cannon might be 4/13/4/3. A pure tank: 15/2/3/4. Pick your poison.

## In the dungeon

- **Rooms generate procedurally.** No two runs look the same.
- **Smash everything.** Crates, barrels, the occasional confused goblin.
- **Pickups** show up: weapon upgrades, coins, beer.
- **Coins** are yours. Coin counter tracks bragging rights.
- **One player down?** Others keep going. You can still trash-talk the corpse.
- **Everyone down?** Game over, head back, try again.

---

## Beer Time

Between rounds, **drink up.** Yes, it's a thing.

- **Pace yourself** — roughly 100ml every 6 minutes is the optimal pull. Faster gets you crowned.
- **Slam 250ml at once** for a 25% stat boost the next round.
- **Drink the most overall** of any player and you wear **the crown** — visible to everyone. You earned it.
- Everything's tracked: weight, bottle volume, total ml. The game knows.

## Tips

- Bluesky character or guest? You're on the same point budget. **Nobody can pay-to-win.**
- Standing still during combat: bad. Sticks should always be moving.
- The Lightning weapon hits at range but slowly — pair it with high AGI.
- The Knife is fast and brutal up close — pair it with high VIT so you can tank the trade.
- Goop is weird. Try it.
- The crown is a real thing. It's a real thing.
